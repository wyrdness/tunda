# Sources: La Tunda

### Anthropological and Ethnographic Studies

1. **Velásquez, Rogerio** (1959). "Cuentos de la raza negra." *Revista Colombiana de Folclor*, Vol. 2, No. 4, pp. 147-198.

## Field Research Recommendations

Researchers planning field work should:
- Contact local community organizations first
- Work with established cultural centers
- Engage community members as research partners
- Respect intellectual property and cultural protocols
- Ensure research benefits local communities
- Document with permission and appropriate attribution

## Notes on Sources

Many oral traditions have not been published in formal academic sources but exist in:
- Community archives
- Personal collections of elders
- Local cultural centers
- Regional museums
- School educational materials
- Family storytelling traditions

Researchers should prioritize community-based sources and ensure ethical engagement with living cultural traditions. La Tunda is not merely a subject of academic study but an active part of Afro-Colombian cultural identity and practice.
